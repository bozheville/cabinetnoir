Vigenère, Beaufort and Autokey share one 26 × 26 table: every row is the alphabet shifted one more place. A key letter picks the row, the message letter picks the column.

Key LEMON over ATTACK: A under L gives L, T under E gives X, T under M gives F… ATTACK becomes LXFOPV.

A repeating key repeats its patterns. Kasiski spacing and the index of coincidence give the key length; each column then falls to plain frequency analysis.
